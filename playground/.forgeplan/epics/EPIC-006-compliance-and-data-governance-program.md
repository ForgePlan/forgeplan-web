# EPIC-006: Compliance and data governance program

## Vision

Helios meets SOC2 Type II by 2027-Q1, GDPR + CCPA + APPI by 2026-Q4,
and ships customer-facing audit log + DSAR workflows. Compliance is
not just a checkbox — the controls become product capabilities the
customer benefits from.

## Strategic context

EVID-011 surfaced 7 critical gaps blocking SOC2 Type II observation
period. Enterprise prospects routinely ask for our SOC2 report;
without it, ~30% of pipeline is gated.
